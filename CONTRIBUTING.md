# Contributing

This project welcomes implementation feedback, corrections, examples, and proposals from anyone working with stereoscopic media.

## Reporting an issue

Please include:

- the specification section or property involved;
- the behavior you expected;
- the behavior you observed; and
- a minimal metadata example when possible.

Do not attach private photographs merely to demonstrate their metadata. A reduced XMP packet is usually sufficient.

## Evolution policy

Version 1.0 is stable. Editorial corrections that do not change meaning may be recorded as errata.

A compatible 1.x revision may register additional open-choice values or optional properties when the existing data model is sufficient. Existing meanings, ordering contracts, and defaults will not change within the 1.x namespace.

An incompatible semantic or structural change requires a new major version and namespace. Deprecated terms remain documented and are never silently reassigned.

Proposals should explain the concrete interchange problem, why native container metadata does not already solve it, and how older readers can safely handle the addition.
