# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-07

- Established the `https://stereoscopy.org/ns/stereo/1.0/` namespace.
- Defined `Format`, ordered `ViewSet`, and per-view `Perspective`, `Primary`, `Synthesized`, and `AnaglyphComponent` properties.
- Registered side-by-side, over-under, anaglyph, Google VR, Apple spatial, and CIPA DC-006 format values.
- Registered red, cyan, green, magenta, amber, and blue anaglyph component values.
